# three.js_Solor_System
Experience the Solar System like never before! This realistic Three.js simulation accelerates time at 1 hour = 1 second or Real Time, allowing you to observe planetary motion in an engaging and interactive way.

Select any planet to instantly focus on it, explore detailed information, and experience real-time shadows and lighting for greater realism. Zoom in to inspect planets up close, control the simulation speed, drag to rotate the camera, scroll to zoom, and use right-drag for free navigation. With realistic textures, accurate orbits, and many more interactive features, there's always something new to discover.

Educational Purpose

This project is designed to help students and space enthusiasts better understand how our Solar System works through interactive visualization. By observing the positions and movements of the Earth, Moon, and Sun in accelerated time, students can clearly see how a solar eclipse (Surya Grahan) and a lunar eclipse (Chandra Grahan) occur. Watching these events in a realistic 3D environment makes it much easier to understand the alignment of celestial bodies than with static images or diagrams.

Whether you're learning astronomy or simply exploring space, this simulation provides an engaging and educational way to visualize planetary motion and eclipse events.
